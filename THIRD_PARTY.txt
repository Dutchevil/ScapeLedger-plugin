ScapeLedger Windows development distribution

The bundled lib directory contains the RuneLite client and its runtime dependencies,
resolved by Gradle from https://repo.runelite.net and Maven Central. JARs are kept intact,
including their META-INF licenses, notices and service descriptors. BUILD.json records
exact coordinates and file hashes. No game cache, user credentials or JRE is bundled.
